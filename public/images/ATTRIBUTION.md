# Image attribution and licensing
Add one row per photo. Use only images whose license allows commercial use, check for visible people/logos/watermarks,
and keep proof of the license. Photos of identifiable people should come from sources that cover model releases.
Do not present stock "before/after" images as real clinic results; keep the "illustrative" labels.

without attribution (attribution is recorded here anyway). Every photo was verified on its source page before
download on 2026-11-18. No AI-generated images were used. The smile gallery images are stock close-up smiles
shown only with the site's existing "Before (illustrative)" / "After (illustrative)" labels — they are not
presented as real clinic patients or results.
